STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE — SOP-SAF-014
Title: Hot Work Permit and Execution Procedure
Revision: 4 (2024-01-15)
Owner: HSE Department
Applies to: All units, including Unit 2 (Gas Compression) and Unit 3 (Crude Distillation)

1. PURPOSE
To control the risks of hot work (welding, grinding, cutting) in areas where flammable
hydrocarbons may be present, in compliance with PESO licensing requirements and the
Factory Act 1948.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all hot work outside designated workshops. Equipment commonly affected includes
pumps (e.g. P-101), pressure vessels (e.g. V-200), and compressors (e.g. C-150).

3. PERMIT REQUIREMENTS
3.1 A Hot Work Permit must be issued by the Area Authority before work begins.
3.2 Gas testing must confirm < 10% LEL before and during the work.
3.3 Hazardous area classification per PESO must be reviewed; work in Zone 1/Zone 2
    areas requires additional controls.
3.4 A trained fire watch must be present during work and for 30 minutes afterward.

4. PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE)
Flame-resistant clothing, welding shield, gloves, and respiratory protection as required by
the job hazard analysis. PPE compliance is auditable under the Factory Act.

5. ISOLATION
All affected equipment must be isolated under the Permit-to-Work and lockout-tagout (LOTO)
system before hot work commences.

6. RESPONSIBILITIES
- Area Authority (A. Mehta): permit issue and area control.
- HSE Inspector (S. Nair): gas testing verification and PPE audit.
- Executing Technician (R. Sharma): compliance with permit conditions.

7. RECORDS
Completed permits are retained for audit per OISD and Factory Act recordkeeping requirements.
