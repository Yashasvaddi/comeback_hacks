Section 7 — Bearing and Lubrication Maintenance

7.1 BEARING SPECIFICATION
Drive-end and non-drive-end bearings: SKF 6310 deep-groove ball bearings.
Maximum continuous operating temperature: 80 degC.
Recommended vibration limit: 4.5 mm/s RMS per ISO 10816-3 for this frame size.

7.2 LUBRICATION SCHEDULE
For continuous duty above 60 degC ambient, re-grease bearings every 3 months using
Shell Gadus S2 V220 or equivalent NLGI 2 lithium-complex grease. Over-greasing causes
churning and heat; under-greasing accelerates spalling.

7.3 ALIGNMENT
Shaft-to-driver alignment must be verified with a laser alignment tool after any coupling
work. Acceptable parallel misalignment: <= 0.05 mm. Misalignment is a leading cause of
premature bearing wear and seal failure.

7.4 MECHANICAL SEAL
Mechanical seal type: John Crane Type 21. Inspect seal faces for scoring during any bearing
service. Replace if face wear exceeds 0.1 mm.

7.5 CONDITION MONITORING
Trend bearing temperature and vibration continuously. A sustained rise in vibration above
4.5 mm/s RMS indicates developing bearing or alignment problems and warrants inspection.

7.6 WARNING
Operation with known misalignment or degraded lubrication will void the bearing warranty and
significantly shorten service life.
