INCIDENT INVESTIGATION REPORT — INC-2024-0731
Classification: Process Safety Incident (High Severity)
Date of Incident: 2024-07-28, 03:42 hrs
Location: Unit 2 — Gas Compression Area
Asset Tag: C-150
Equipment: Reciprocating Compressor, Ariel JGK/4
Reported by: Night Shift Operator (P. Verma); Investigated by: A. Mehta (Maintenance Engineer) and S. Nair (Inspector)

SUMMARY
At 03:42 hrs, Reciprocating Compressor C-150 tripped on high discharge temperature
(154 degC against a 150 degC trip setpoint). A subsequent inspection found a cracked
discharge valve plate on the 2nd-stage cylinder and evidence of lubricant carbonization.
No injuries occurred; the unit was safely isolated under the Permit-to-Work system.

SEQUENCE OF EVENTS
- 2024-07-26: Inspection logged rod-drop at 0.48 mm (threshold 0.50 mm) — flagged as a watch item.
- 2024-07-28 02:10: Discharge temperature began trending upward from 142 degC.
- 2024-07-28 03:42: High-temperature trip activated; compressor shut down automatically.
- 2024-07-28 04:30: Area isolated, locked out, and tagged out (PTW-4102).

IMMEDIATE CAUSE
Cracked 2nd-stage discharge valve plate causing internal gas recirculation and heat buildup.

CONTRIBUTING FACTORS
- Valve plates had exceeded the OEM-recommended 8,000-hour replacement interval (last replaced at
  9,200 running hours).
- Lubricant degradation reduced cooling effectiveness; oil analysis showed high TAN (total acid number).
- The rising rod-drop trend was noted but not escalated to a work order.

ROOT CAUSES
- Inadequate valve-plate replacement schedule for high-duty reciprocating service.
- No automated escalation linking condition-monitoring alarms to the maintenance work-order system.

RECOMMENDATIONS
- Reduce 2nd-stage valve-plate replacement interval to 6,000 hours for C-150.
- Add automated alarm-to-work-order escalation for rod-drop and discharge-temperature trends.
- Include oil TAN trending in the monthly condition-monitoring review.

REGULATORY NOTES
Investigated and documented per OISD-STD-118 (incident reporting and investigation).
Permit-to-Work executed under Factory Act 1948 requirements.
