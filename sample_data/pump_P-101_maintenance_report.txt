MAINTENANCE WORK ORDER — WO-2024-0512
Asset Tag: P-101
Equipment: Centrifugal Pump, Model KSB Etanorm 080-065-200
Location: Unit 3 — Crude Distillation Area
Date: 2024-05-12
Performed by: R. Sharma (Mechanical Technician), supervised by A. Mehta (Maintenance Engineer)

PROBLEM REPORTED
Operators reported abnormal vibration and intermittent high bearing temperature
on Pump P-101 over the preceding two weeks. Vibration trend exceeded 7.1 mm/s RMS,
above the 4.5 mm/s alarm threshold per ISO 10816-3.

WORK PERFORMED
1. Pump isolated, locked out, and tagged out (LOTO permit PTW-3391).
2. Drive-end and non-drive-end bearings (SKF 6310) removed and inspected.
3. Drive-end bearing showed spalling on the outer race — confirmed bearing wear.
4. Mechanical seal (John Crane Type 21) inspected; light scoring on seal faces.
5. Both bearings replaced; mechanical seal replaced.
6. Shaft alignment checked with laser alignment tool; corrected to 0.05 mm.
7. Pump re-commissioned; post-repair vibration measured at 2.3 mm/s RMS.

ROOT CAUSE (PRELIMINARY)
Bearing wear accelerated by misalignment introduced during the previous coupling
replacement in WO-2024-0188 (February 2024). Contributing factor: inadequate
lubrication interval — grease replenishment was on a 6-month cycle versus the
OEM-recommended 3-month cycle for this duty.

RECOMMENDATIONS
- Revise lubrication PM frequency for P-101 to 3 months.
- Add laser alignment verification as a mandatory step after any coupling work.
- Trend bearing temperature via the existing condition-monitoring system.

PARTS USED
- SKF 6310 bearing x2
- John Crane Type 21 mechanical seal x1
- Shell Gadus S2 V220 grease

COMPLIANCE
Work carried out under OISD-STD-105 (maintenance of rotating equipment) and the
plant Permit-to-Work system per Factory Act requirements.
